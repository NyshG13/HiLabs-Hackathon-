Error analysis

The bar is 95%. This is where we miss.

∅

Never in the pool

For 5.7% of ICD and 10.4% of SNOMED rows the right code is never retrieved, so no later stage can find it. Retrieval recall caps accuracy.

∞

The long tail

Codes unseen in training are mapped correctly 62.8% of the time on the internal test. That is reasoning, not memorising, and it is the weakest slice.

⌗

Specificity slips

“fx of right distal radius” maps to S52.91XB (open fracture, whole forearm) instead of the closed distal-radius code: a confident, wrong specificity call.

Low-confidence rows are flagged in every upload (confidence under 0.5) so a reviewer sees them first. Numbers come from the TermMap-X+ repository’s own evaluation: internal blind test, 243 ICD and 275 SNOMED diagnosis rows, scored once.

⌂

Stays on your machine

Models run locally or on your own GPU. No hosted API, no keys, no PHI leaving the building.

✓

Always a legal code

Every emitted code is checked against the supplied dictionaries, for both systems, on every row.

◐

Knows what it doesn’t know

A calibrated confidence per system, and the lower of the two, on every row.
